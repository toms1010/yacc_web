About YACC

The Youth Annual Conference for Christ (YACC) is a gathering of young believers from Churches of Christ across the region. Our mission is to inspire, equip, and connect the next generation of Christian leaders through worship, teaching, and fellowship.

This year's theme "CONNECT" emphasizes our call to connect with God, with each other, and with our purpose in Christ.

YACC has been transforming lives for over 20 years, and we invite you to be part of this life-changing experience!

About the Venue

The 2025 Youth Annual Conference for Christ will be held in Iloilo City Proper, Iloilo City, Iloilo.

Address: Iloilo City Proper, Iloilo City, Iloilo, Philippines

The venue features:

  • Spacious auditorium for main sessions
  • Multiple breakout rooms for workshops
  • Beautiful outdoor spaces for fellowship
  • On-site dining facilities
  • Safe and secure environment

Accommodations are available at nearby hotels and dormitories. Please contact the registration team for recommendations.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the age range for YACC?

YACC is designed for youth aged 13-25, but all are welcome to attend.

Is accommodation provided?

Accommodation is not included, but we provide recommendations for nearby hotels and dormitories.

What should I bring?

Bring your Bible, a notebook, comfortable clothing, and an open heart ready to connect!

Are meals provided?

Meals are provided as indicated in the schedule. Please inform us of any dietary restrictions during registration.
